Our story

Rooted in nature.
Refined by craft.

We cook the way the seasons arrive — patiently, with attention to texture, temperature, and the quiet detail that turns a good meal into a memorable one. EMBER & OAK is a small contemporary dining room built around a single idea: that great ingredients, handled with care, deserve time, space, and good company.

Our menus change with the market and the weather. We favour producers who grow things slowly, and we finish every plate with the calm precision that only unhurried kitchens allow.

Culinary philosophy

Cook simply. Finish precisely.

We believe the best dishes are the result of restraint — fewer elements, better sourced, given the attention they deserve. No theatrics. No excess. Just ingredients treated with honest technique and served while they still taste of where they came from.

Our kitchen is led by a tight team who cook the way they would for people they love.
